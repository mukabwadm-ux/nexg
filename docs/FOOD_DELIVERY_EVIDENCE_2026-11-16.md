## Minimal pre-pagination contract needed next
Do not apply the exact-item reader to a paginated search page. A future source-local candidate RPC must (1) resolve the complete public Food item/primary-branch universe under the same query/category/subcategory scope, (2) evaluate explicit destination + route/hours predicates before cursor/limit, (3) keep unknown excluded from positive matches but separately counted, (4) return deterministic ordering/cursor and evidence tied to source snapshot/request/destination, without persisting coordinates, and (5) fail closed with an explicit incomplete/unsupported capability if a bounded evaluation cannot cover the universe. It cannot promise stock or checkout availability. Current Discovery catalogue is an in-memory snapshot, while source hours and routing are live reads: merging an incomplete live page into snapshot pagination would produce incorrect totals and missing matches. Hence no list filter is enabled by this branch binding.

Pickup proof is specifically about the currently checked web checkout path: merchant onboarding declares customer_pickup, but fn_web_order_quote has no corresponding selected fulfillment mode. General workflow schema policy definitions do not establish a published per-item pickup assignment and executable checkout contract. A future pickup contract needs explicit published item/branch assignment, customer-collection mode, effective pickup hours/lead time, and matching checkout selection. This does not question or replace the separate reservation engine's authority.

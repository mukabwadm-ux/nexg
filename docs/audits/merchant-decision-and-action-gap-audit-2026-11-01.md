# Merchant decision and action gap audit

1 November 2026, Africa/Nairobi. Source and design review. Forty findings.

The portal has substantial interactive frontend work. Work has local next-step controls and automatic-confirmation examples. Offerings, Availability, Business, Money and Promotions have meaningful sample interactions, source boundaries and recovery mechanisms. The gaps below describe what remains before those experiences can support dependable merchant tasks and demonstrate usefulness.

This audit uses the newer working files in `C:/Users/limta/Desktop/MERCHANT WORKSPACE`, including uncommitted changes, plus the 1 November Insights concept in `C:/Users/limta/Documents/ChatGPT/NEXG MERCHANT PORTA`. The older Documents application code is not treated as the latest frontend. The reviewed Git heads were `c92c02dd1d0f4ad36f09034293d52e8d6e1c6d8a` in Desktop and `c474d21ba4f81f1ce6471273c5ffa9a25cc1fa01` in Documents; the heads alone do not include the uncommitted work.

This was a read-only review of product code, source adapters, relevant migrations, design contracts, acceptance records, handoffs and checks. It did not exercise a deployed site, query live merchant data, interview merchants, send messages, execute payments or change application code. Source-visible defects are distinguished from intentionally deferred delivery and missing validation evidence. A documented preview limit is not a failure to meet an earlier frontend-only assignment. Findings are complete for the reviewed decision/action scope, not a claim that every possible runtime defect has been found.

P1 means close before merchants rely on the affected real operation or figure. P2 means a consequential task, relevance or validation gap. P3 means an audit or preview-coherence gap. Classification names are: **implementation defect**, **delivery gap**, **design gap**, and **validation gap**. Proposed product/technical owners below are assignments to make, not claims that named teams already exist.

1. **G01 · P2 · Validation gap · Merchant needs remain assumptions without recorded observation.** The reviewed plans describe personas and future merchant research, but the reviewed audit, handoff and acceptance records do not document merchants performing their own consequential tasks in their normal working conditions. Category coverage cannot establish which problems are frequent or costly. The [implementation plan](<C:/Users/limta/Desktop/MERCHANT WORKSPACE/docs/merchant-portal-plan/implementation-plan.md:22>) still places measured user tasks in a future pilot. Close when the product/research owner records representative roles, operating models, actual incidents, current workarounds and observed task outcomes, including mobile and mixed businesses.

2. **G02 · P2 · Design gap · Metrics do not require a merchant decision contract.** The [required metric fields](<C:/Users/limta/Documents/ChatGPT/NEXG MERCHANT PORTA/docs/merchant-portal-plan/insights-concept/metric-registry.json:14>) specify the question, calculation, evidence, technical owner and drill-down. They do not require the person making a decision, the triggering business situation, the available action and a success/follow-up measure. A correct report can therefore pass specification checks while changing no useful decision. Close when product and the relevant merchant role define these fields for each promoted report or observation and validate them against an actual task. Do not invent thresholds before that evidence exists.

3. **G03 · P2 · Validation gap · The moment of use is not established for each role.** Report periods and operational views exist, but the reviewed acceptance work does not establish when a counter operator, location manager, owner or finance user needs each one in an existing routine. The [Insights journeys](<C:/Users/limta/Documents/ChatGPT/NEXG MERCHANT PORTA/docs/merchant-portal-plan/insights-concept/screen-design.md:151>) describe navigation rather than observed operating habits; [scheduled report delivery is explicitly deferred](<C:/Users/limta/Documents/ChatGPT/NEXG MERCHANT PORTA/docs/merchant-portal-plan/insights-concept/screen-design.md:121>). Close when product validates the actual trigger, frequency and useful channel per task. A successful occasional decision must not require daily dashboard use.

4. **G04 · P2 · Design and validation gap · We cannot yet measure the portal's contribution to task success.** The [statistics collection contract](<C:/Users/limta/Desktop/MERCHANT WORKSPACE/docs/merchant-portal-plan/stats/collection-contract.md:13>) defines business facts. No contract was found in the inspected merchant frontend and acceptance packages connecting an eligible problem, a displayed finding, the merchant's action, owner confirmation and subsequent result. Existing domain events and audit history can supply parts of this evidence; an additional analytics vendor is not a requirement. Close when product and engineering define minimal permitted evaluation evidence and task-specific denominators, then verify that successful resolution and abandonment can be distinguished.

5. **G05 · P2 · Validation gap · No measured merchant baseline or adoption result is established.** Existing checks provide useful layout, calculation, state and interaction evidence. For example, the [Work smoke checks](<C:/Users/limta/Desktop/MERCHANT WORKSPACE/scripts/smoke-work-preview.cjs:15>) inspect a scripted fixture, while the [statistics acceptance plan](<C:/Users/limta/Desktop/MERCHANT WORKSPACE/docs/merchant-portal-plan/stats/frontend-and-acceptance.md:59>) distinguishes mapping checks from runtime acceptance. They do not establish the merchant's prior resolution time, errors, assistance needs or relevant business outcome. Close when product records a baseline, observes the same real tasks during a pilot, and agrees useful success/stop criteria and follow-up ownership. Login counts alone do not close this gap.

6. **G06 · P2 · Design gap · An insight has no required follow-up through its eventual result.** The [Insights observation and suggestion rules](<C:/Users/limta/Documents/ChatGPT/NEXG MERCHANT PORTA/docs/merchant-portal-plan/insights-concept/screen-design.md:79>) correctly require evidence and avoid invented causes. They do not require recording whether a proposed investigation was useful, what the merchant chose, or whether a later result supported the decision. Close, when suggestions are introduced, with a proportionate follow-up method linking the evidence and chosen action to confirmed outcomes or a merchant explanation. Keep observations, hypotheses and proven effects distinct; do not infer causal improvement from a before/after chart alone.

# NexG: notes for App Store and Google Play review

> **For the owner, not for Apple:** paste the section "Review notes" into App Store Connect (App Review Information → Notes) and the Play Console (App access → instructions). Before submitting:
> - Create the review account and add it to `payment_reviewer`.
> - Fill in its email and password below, in the store consoles only, never in this file.
> - Check that the things listed in `PLAN.md` ("Still open") are done, especially the app showing the review shops to the review account.

---

## Review notes

**Sign in.** Use the review account given in the sign-in fields of this submission. Every payment made with it is a Paystack **test** payment: no money moves, and the receipt says so.

**Delivery address.** You don't need GPS. When asked, choose the area **Westlands, Nairobi** and enter any street, for example "Woodvale Grove".

**Paying.** Use one of these:
- **Test card:** `4084 0840 8408 4081`, any future expiry date, CVV `408`.
- **Test M-Pesa number:** `+254 710 000 000`.

**The shops.** They are open 24 hours, every day. Each section below names a shop: open it from its category on Home, or search for its name.

### Shopping

**1. Order food (add to cart, choices, offer).** Food & Restaurants → **Kijiji Grill House**.
1. Tap **Nyama Choma Platter**.
2. Choose a side and any extras, then tap **Add to cart**.
3. Open the cart and tap **Checkout**.
4. Choose delivery or pickup, now or later, then pay.

The platter shows a 15% lunch offer.

**2. Groceries sold by weight.** Groceries → **Shamba Fresh Market**.
1. Tap **Tomatoes**, choose **1.5 kg** and add to the cart. The price is confirmed after weighing.
2. Check out.

"Brown Eggs" shows as sold out.

**3. Clothing and shoes (size and colour).** Fashion → **Hatua Footwear & Apparel**.
1. Tap **Classic Canvas Sneakers** and pick a size and colour. Size 44 in White is sold out.
2. Add to the cart and check out.

On **Cotton Crew T-Shirt**, XL costs more.

**4. eSIM (digital, no delivery).** Tech → **Mtandao Mobile**.
1. Tap **Kenya eSIM 5 GB, 7 days** and add it to the cart.
2. At checkout, enter any passport details (for example GB, X1234567) and pay.

The activation code appears in **Activity**.

**5. Gift with a message.** Flowers & Gifts → **Sukari Cakes & Gifts**.
1. Tap **Chocolate Fudge Cake 1 kg** and type a message for the top.
2. Add to the cart and check out.

**6. Home goods.** Marketplace → **Nyumba Living**: add any item and check out.

### Bookings

**7. Appointment (choose a therapist and a time).** Beauty & Wellness → **Utulivu Spa & Fitness**.
1. Tap **Swedish Massage, 60 min**.
2. Pick a therapist, a date and a free time.
3. Tap **Book** and pay.

**8. Membership plan.** Same shop: tap **Monthly Gym Membership**, choose a start date, **Join**, then pay.

**9. Event tickets with ticket types.** Experiences → **Sauti Live Events**.
1. Tap **Sunset Acoustic Evening**. It runs every evening at 18:30.
2. Choose a date and **Regular** or **VIP**, and how many.
3. Tap **Book tickets** and pay.

**10. Cinema seats.** Experiences → Movies → **Nyota Cinemas**.
1. Choose a film and a showtime.
2. Pick seats on the seat map, hold them and pay.

**11. Court reservation.** Play → **Uwanja Courts & Karting**.
1. Tap **Padel Court, 1 hour**.
2. Choose a date, a free time and the number of players.
3. Tap **Reserve**.

**12. Timed session ticket.** Same shop: tap **Go-Karting, 15-minute session**, pick a session and book.

**13. Table reservation.** Food & Restaurants → **Kijiji Grill House**.
1. Tap **Reserve a table** at the top of the shop.
2. Choose a date, a time and the number of guests, then tap **Reserve**.

There is no charge to reserve.

**14. Tour with departures.** Travel & Tours → **Mjini Safaris & Tours**.
1. Tap **Nairobi City Highlights Tour**. It departs daily at 09:00 and 14:00.
2. Choose a departure and the number of travellers, then book and pay.

**15. Airport transfer.** Airport Transfers → choose **JKIA → Westlands** → **Karibu Airport Transfers**.
1. Tap **Sedan: JKIA to Westlands**.
2. Enter a pickup time, then book and pay.

**16. Car or bike hire.** Car Hire → **Gari Car Hire**.
1. Tap **Toyota Axio (Sedan)**.
2. Choose pickup and return dates, self-drive or with a driver.
3. Reserve and pay.

The refundable deposit is shown before you pay.

**17. Service with drop-off and collection windows.** Laundry & Cleaning → **Safi Laundry & Cleaning**.
1. Tap **Wash & Fold, per kg**.
2. Choose a drop-off window, a collection window and the weight.
3. Book and pay.

**18. Service at your address.** Same shop: tap **Home Cleaning, 3 hours**, choose a time, enter a Westlands address and book.

**19. Quote, then reserve with a 50% deposit.** Experiences → **Sauti Live Events**.
1. Tap **Private Event Package**.
2. Describe the event (date, guests, budget).
3. Tap **Reserve with deposit**.

The deposit is half of the top of the shown price range (KES 75,000), as a test payment. The same flow works at:
- **Faraja Concierge** (any service);
- **Anga Air Charters** (any charter);
- **Gari Car Hire** (Corporate Fleet Hire).

### After ordering or booking

Every order and booking appears in **Activity** with its stages. Review orders are completed by our staff, and no real rider is dispatched.

**Account deletion:** Account → Settings → Privacy → Delete account.

# Customer offering screens across all categories

Status: design specification for all current product and service kinds. [Category compositions](../offering-map/all-screens-and-workflows.md) and [structured field/screen/workflow bindings](../offering-map/all-screens-and-workflows.json) supply the actual selected offering fields.

## CS_BROWSE: Browse and search

Show actual product identity, accurate media, selected meaningful highlights and correctly labelled price/availability. Do not squeeze all specifications onto the card.

## CS_DETAIL: Offering detail and specifications

Group actual item-specific facts, inclusions and source-backed descriptors with labels/units. Missing, unknown and not-applicable states are distinct; private records and internal IDs are absent.

## CS_OPTIONS: Variant, option and quantity selection

Offer real combinations and required/optional compatible choices. Selection updates the actual specification override, image, price and availability rather than silently substituting another item.

## CS_FIT: Size guide or fit information

Only where relevant, show the actual chart/fit meaning, selected row, units and measurement basis. Never invent a measurement or cross-system equivalence.

## CS_PRICE: Price and charge breakdown

Show the selected price and actual unit/pack/quantity/period/person/quote basis, applicable extras and total. Starting estimates, deposits and unpriced requests retain their actual meaning.

## CS_FULFILMENT: Delivery, collection or service location

Show supported methods, exact location eligibility and authoritative estimates/promises. Unknown service coverage and freshness do not become a guaranteed delivery.

## CS_SCHEDULE: Date, time or capacity selection

Render only the actual selected model's supported schedule/interval/occurrence/paired-window behavior. Confirmation depends on source authority and all applicable constraints.

## CS_TERMS: Terms and eligibility

Show applicable approved return/cancellation/deposit/usage/eligibility/renewal/provider terms before commitment. A seller answer does not establish verification or permission.

## CS_REQUEST: Private order, booking or enquiry input

Collect only necessary purpose-specific inputs and customer choices for this offering/model. Public form definitions and private responses remain distinct. Show pending versus accepted outcomes accurately.

## CS_REVIEW: Review and confirmation

Review the actual selected item/variant/options, quantity/unit or interval, accepted price and policy references before the owner command. Confirmation, allocation, payment, fulfilment and notifications remain separate outcomes.

## CS_TRACKING: Order, booking or request progress

Show the existing selected workflow's actual states and permitted customer actions from authoritative work. Quotes, requests, production, rentals, entitlements and provider handoffs do not become generic delivery orders.

## Acceptance

For each selected offering kind, verify the actual capture-to-public-projection and selection path; preserve real variants/units/compatibility, accurate price and source-confirmed availability, private intake boundaries and immutable accepted specification/policy references. Render the registered workflow's request/confirmation/tracking meaning. Include mobile, keyboard, errors, unavailable choices and stale/conflicting sources in the future implementation evidence.

The current artifacts specify the behavior and verify design references; they do not implement or certify working application screens.

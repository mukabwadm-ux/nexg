# Adaptive Insights concept

Date: 1 November 2026. Status: proposed experience and illustrative concept images. No application implementation or live reporting change.

Insights answers a merchant's questions about discovery, enquiries, accepted work, fulfilment, capacity, offering performance, retention, promotions and recorded money. The screen composes reports from the merchant's actual enabled activities, selected offering models, available evidence and permissions. Category identity alone never enables a report.

The scope preserves all 21 categories and 128 baseline subcategories, plus the 21 previously approved additional activity routes. These are additional offering/model routes within existing specialties, not 21 new merchant categories. Fifteen operational models compose the experience.

Read the [screen design](screen-design.md), [metric definitions](metric-registry.json), [explicit merchant coverage](coverage.md), [structured coverage](coverage.json), [implementation and validation plan](validation-plan.md), and [image gallery](images.md). The [exact image prompts](image-prompts.json), [image provenance](image-manifest.json) and [verification record](verification.json) keep the concept reproducible.

The twelve image scenes show a mixed business overview, product orders, appointments, rentals, spaces, quotes and production, shipment operations, sessions and entitlements, promotion attribution, recorded financial activity, a metric drill-down, and mobile partial data. They demonstrate distinct reporting questions and units. The coverage matrix specifies the variants that do not require separate pictures.

All names, numbers, records and connections in the pictures are fictional review fixtures. Pictures establish visual intent only. The written definitions govern calculations, interactions and data boundaries. Existing application analytics remain separate and delivery-centric; extending them requires the source, policy and authorization work in the validation plan.

This detailed concept extends [SC_INSIGHTS](../screens/07-insights.md) and inherits the [shared screen contract](../screen-contract.md). It preserves the current reconciled booking and offering definitions. It does not alter pricing, accepted work, publication, payout or clinical/provider policy.

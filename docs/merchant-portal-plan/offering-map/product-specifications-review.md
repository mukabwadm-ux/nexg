The exact templates also specify applicable required/optional conditions, unit/value shape, offering/variant/location/transaction scope, source ownership and privacy. Personal customer data appears only in the private request/transaction route; a private destination is not a public product specification.

The information is carried through all eight merchant screens and eleven customer surfaces. Accepted work binds the actual selected specification, options, quantity/unit and policy/price versions to the existing selected workflow. Current listing changes cannot rewrite accepted history.
